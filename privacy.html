Privacy policy

Field Notes. Last updated 20 November 2026.

What the app does with Google data

Field Notes signs in to one Google account: the team owner's own. It uses Google Drive only to upload field recordings and their transcripts into a folder in that account, and to read the IDs of the files it uploaded so the notes can link to them.

What it stores and shares

  • Recordings and transcripts stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from other Google users is accessed.

Removing data

The owner can delete the files from Google Drive at any time and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
